1. A Scan identifies a bottle by its barcode, by label text read on the phone or by a photo of the label. To read photos and text, the App uses an artificial intelligence model from a third-party provider (Anthropic, Claude model). On the Premium Plan the App may also search the internet for information about the bottle.
2. Scan results, including the description of taste and aroma (tasting notes), are for information only. They may be incomplete or wrong. The App marks values that come from the model's knowledge as “to be confirmed”. The User can correct them before saving.
3. Information in the App does not replace the producer's label. This applies in particular to alcohol content, ingredients and allergens.
4. The App accepts only photos of alcoholic beverages, including their alcohol-free versions (e.g. 0.0 beer). It may reject a photo of another product, a photo of several different bottles or an unreadable photo.
5. The number of Scans is limited by a daily limit that depends on the plan (§6). Rejected Scans also count towards the limit. Short-term restrictions may also result from abuse protection (e.g. too many Scans within a minute).
6. Label photos are stored on the Provider's server. They are used as the bottle thumbnail in the Cellar and to improve label recognition. Details are in the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
